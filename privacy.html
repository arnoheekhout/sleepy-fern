Privacy Policy

Sleepy Fern Uploader · last updated 22 October 2026

Sleepy Fern Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used by the owner of the Sleepy Fern YouTube channel to upload and manage videos on that one channel. It is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • Data of the channel owner's own YouTube channel: channel details, the channel's videos, playlists, thumbnails, titles, descriptions and translations.
  • Statistics of those videos from YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting (views, watch time, impressions, click-through rate).
  • Public information about other public videos returned by YouTube search (titles, view counts, thumbnails), used only to compare our own titles and thumbnails with what is already out there.

The app does not access, collect or store personal data of viewers or of any other YouTube user.

How the data is used

Only to publish and schedule our own videos, fill playlists, test titles and thumbnails, and see which videos viewers enjoy.

To help write our own titles and judge our own thumbnails, the titles and thumbnails of public videos returned by YouTube search are sent, together with our own drafts, to an AI text and image service (OpenCode) that processes them on our behalf. This contains no personal data, and we do not use it to train AI models. Apart from that, nothing is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Data is stored locally on the channel owner's computer. The sign-in token is kept in a local file and can be deleted at any time. Video statistics are kept for as long as the channel is active. Data from other public videos is refreshed regularly and stored data older than 30 days is replaced or deleted.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the Sleepy Fern channel page.
